About Me

ECFMG-certified international medical graduate and licensed general practitioner in India, with clinical experience across academic hospital wards, primary care, ICU, and diverse healthcare settings. Complemented by 7 months of hands-on US clinical experience in outpatient and inpatient settings—taking vitals, histories, and complaints, documenting across EMR systems including EPIC, eClinicalWorks, Oracle Cerner, CureMD, and ProVation, participating in minor procedures and case presentations, and coordinating with medical assistants and nurse practitioners as part of the care team. Actively engaged in research, with peer-reviewed publications and ongoing projects. Alongside medicine, I’ve mentored juniors through their USMLE journey, led university events as a student leader, and worked with an NGO-run mobile medical unit to provide primary care to underserved rural populations. My journey across India, Russia, and the U.S. has helped me appreciate different cultures, perspectives, and approaches to healthcare. Outside of medicine, I enjoy paragliding, rafting, and chasing sunsets in the mountains—adventures that keep me energized and connected to life beyond the clinic.
