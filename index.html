About Me

ECFMG-certified international medical graduate and licensed general practitioner in India, having worked independently across academic hospital wards, primary care, and ICU settings. Complemented by 6 months of hands-on US clinical experience in both outpatient and inpatient settings— taking vitals, histories, and complaints, documenting across EMR systems including EPIC, eClinicalWorks, CureMD, Sarna, and ProVation, participating in minor procedures and case presentations, and coordinating with medical assistants and nurse practitioners as part of the care team. Actively engaged in research, with peer-reviewed publications and ongoing projects. Alongside medicine, I've mentored juniors through their USMLE journey, led university events as a student leader, and worked with an NGO-run mobile medical unit to provide primary care to underserved rural populations. Outside of medicine, I enjoy hiking, cooking, and traveling— things that keep me balanced and connected to life beyond the clinic.
